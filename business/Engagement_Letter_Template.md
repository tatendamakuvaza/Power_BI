> [Client] has requested that Maxhub [describe the objective, e.g. "analyse the general ledger for the

## 2. Scope of work

We will perform the following:

1. Obtain and document the data received, including its source, extraction date and integrity checks.
2. Reconcile the data to the trial balance and other control totals provided by you.
3. Perform the agreed analytics programme: [list the tests/procedures].
4. Investigate exceptions to source documents made available by you.
5. Quantify exceptions and distinguish value at risk from quantified loss.
6. Report our findings, and present them to [management / the board / the committee].
7. Hand over the models, working files and documentation.

## 3. Matters not included in scope

- Legal advice, and the provision of expert evidence in proceedings unless separately agreed in writing.
- Tax advice and filings, and any communication with ZIMRA.
- Recovery of funds or pursuing third parties.
- Work on data or periods not specified in this letter.
- Assurance over the completeness or accuracy of systems we can only observe through extracts.

Any work outside this scope will be quoted and agreed in writing before it starts.

## 4. Our responsibilities

We will perform the work with professional skill and care, in accordance with the engagement standards
of [the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Zimbabwe (ICAZ) / the applicable professional body], and
the IESBA Code of Ethics. We will keep you informed of significant findings as they arise rather than
only at the end, and we will raise any indication that the data provided is incomplete or inconsistent.

## 5. Your responsibilities

You will:

1. Provide complete and accurate data and, where relevant, written confirmation that the data is
   complete for the period under review.
2. Provide the data in a machine-readable format within [5] working days of the engagement start date.
3. Nominate a single point of contact authorised to answer queries and provide documents.
4. Make staff available for interviews where required.
5. Ensure that any matter we raise has a decision-maker appointed to act on it.
6. Notify us of any matter that may affect the engagement, including any legal process of which you are
   aware that touches the data.

## 6. Basis of reporting and limitations

Our conclusions are based on the data and documents provided. Data analytics identifies exceptions and
patterns; it does not, by itself, establish intent, and it is not a substitute for document-level
corroboration. Where we cannot corroborate a matter, we will say so. Our report is prepared for
[the addressee] and for the purpose set out in section 1; we accept no responsibility to any other party,
and no third party may rely on it without our written consent.

## 7. Confidentiality and data handling

1. We will keep all information confidential and use it only for this engagement.
2. Access is restricted to the engagement team named below.
3. Data is stored in Maxhub-controlled locations only (encrypted, access-controlled), and mobile copies
   are minimised and deleted after use.
4. We will process personal data lawfully and minimally, in line with applicable data-protection law
   (including Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act (2021)), and will assist you with any data-subject
   request or breach notification that concerns data we hold.
5. On completion and after the retention period stated below, we will [delete / return and delete] the
   engagement data, unless we are required by law or professional standards to retain it.
6. **Retention period:** [7] years for working papers required by professional standards; [90] days for
   source data, unless the matter becomes the subject of legal proceedings, in which case we will hold
   it under legal-hold instructions.
7. If a matter may become subject to legal proceedings, we will where possible work under the direction
   of your legal advisers so that privilege is preserved.
