## The Maxhub commercial rules

1. **Never start work without a signed engagement letter** — the data-handling clause protects the client,
   the scope clause protects you.
2. **Never quote a fee before asking three questions:** what data exists, what decision is waiting, and
   who signs.
3. **Every build proposal carries a retainer line.** One-off projects pay once; retainers pay monthly and
   keep the model alive.
4. **Every deliverable passes an independent review** before it is issued
   (`PowerBI_Build_Standard.md` §7). The reviewer is never the builder.
5. **Price the whole engagement** — mobilisation, build, review, reporting, handover and training.

## The engagement lifecycle (summary)

`Lead → Qualification call → Data sample review → Proposal (fixed scope, fixed fee) → Engagement letter →
Kick-off and data request → Build & analyse → QC review → Report & present → Handover → Retainer offer →
Post-engagement review`

Steps 2 and 3 are where engagements are won or lost: most proposals fail because the scope was guessed,
not because the price was wrong.
