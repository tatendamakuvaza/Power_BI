# capstones/ — four client-ready portfolio projects

Each capstone is a full simulated engagement: brief, deliverables, data, process, rubric and the
commercial wrap (scope, hours, price, and the three questions every client asks).

| Capstone | Simulated client | Deliverable | Sellable as | Fee band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [1 — Forensic Investigation](Capstone_1_Forensic_Investigation.md) | Mhondoro Manufacturing — whistle-blower allegation | Findings pack, quantified loss, evidence register, board presentation | Forensic investigation | $8,000 – $45,000 |
| [2 — Financial Reporting Pack](Capstone_2_Financial_Reporting_Pack.md) | Mhondoro Manufacturing — new FC and a three-week board deadline | Live P&L, BS, cash flow, KPIs, hidden data-quality page | CFO reporting retainer | $4,000 – $15,000 build |
| [3 — Data Analytics Engagement](Capstone_3_Data_Analytics_Engagement.md) | Mhondoro Manufacturing — audit committee assurance | Procurement, receivables, cash analytics suite + committee pack | Internal audit analytics | $6,000 – $18,000 |
| [4 — AI & Advisory Service](Capstone_4_AI_Advisory_Service.md) | Maxhub itself (and its clients) | Churn model, forecast, anomaly detection, governance pack, service blueprint | ML/AI advisory retainer | $6,000 – $30,000 + monthly |

**How to use them**

1. Do Capstone 1 and 2 in order — they reuse Modules 2–10 and build the two assets every prospect asks
   to see.
2. Complete 3 or 4 for breadth (analytics engagement or AI/ML service).
3. Write each up as a portfolio case study: the problem in the client's words, what you did, the number
   you found or saved, how you proved it, and the tool (last).

**Rules for portfolio use**

- Label everything **"simulated engagement data"**. Never present it as a real client.
- Show the reconciliation and the methodology note — that is what separates professionals from
  dashboard builders.
- Redact nothing and invent nothing: the data is fictional, so there is nothing to hide, and honesty
  about that is itself a selling point.
