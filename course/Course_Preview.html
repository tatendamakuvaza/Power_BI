Professional standards built into the course

Technique without standards produces reports nobody can rely on. These rules run through every module, lab and capstone.

Evidence discipline

Keep the original extract, record its hash, analyse a copy, and document every cleaning decision so a third party can reproduce the result.

Language discipline

Report transactions and controls, never people. Separate fact, inference and opinion. Value at risk is not quantified loss — the distinction is stated every time.

Reconciliation discipline

No number is issued until debits equal credits, the balance sheet checks to zero and every headline figure ties to the client's own records.

Quality control

An independent reviewer who did not build the work re-performs a calculation on every page and signs a checklist before the client sees anything.
